How to drive & bonk

Steer with A/D or ←/→. W accelerates; S brakes/reverses. Shift boosts, E drifts, Space fires. On phones, drag the circle sideways to steer. Gas and Brake are on the right.

Follow the arrows through every checkpoint. Race three laps in Circuit, or drive continuously in Endless until your chosen time limit ends. Wrecks cost 30 points and three seconds.
